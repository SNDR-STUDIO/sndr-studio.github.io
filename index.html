01 — About the studioSNDR / Independent creative practice

SNDR is an independent creative studio for motion, digital design, AI-generated content and interactive experiences.

Founded by Alex Savo, SNDR began as a freelance practice in web development and evolved into a multidisciplinary studio. We create visual content for YouTube, TikTok and social platforms, develop characters and visual identities, and build web experiences that feel alive.

Based in Europe. Working worldwide.

05 — How we work

How we work

A clear path from the first message to the final file — no surprises on the way.

01

Brief

A short brief and a 15–20 minute call: goals, audience, references, timing.

02

Concept

Two or three directions with moodboards, styleframes and AI sketches.

03

Production

Animation, 3D, code — with interim previews and 2–3 rounds of edits.

04

Delivery

Final files in every format you need, sources and launch support.
